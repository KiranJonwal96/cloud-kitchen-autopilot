## Signals of a peak
Treat the window as a peak when forecast orders are at least 30% above normal, or a major sports
match, festival or heavy rain is expected during dinner.

## Station overload
When a station is forecast above 100% of its hourly capacity, move one cook from the least-loaded
station 30 minutes before the peak and start batch preparation (see SOP-03). Above 130% of capacity,
also consider pausing the slowest dish of that station for the peak hour.

## Order of preparation
Prepare the highest-volume dishes first and prep garnish and sides for the whole window in advance.
Keep the wok station for fast dishes during the peak.

## Delivery promise
When any station is forecast above capacity, raise the delivery-time estimate on platforms by
10 minutes for the peak window rather than missing promised times.
