## Approval
Every purchase order raised by the autopilot needs approval by the shift supervisor before it is
sent to the supplier.

## Limits
A single purchase order may not exceed INR 25,000. Total procurement per business day may not
exceed INR 60,000. Requests above these limits are escalated to the operations manager.

## Order quantities
Order the shortfall plus safety stock, rounded up to the supplier's order step. Do not order more
than the maximum order quantity set for each ingredient, to limit wastage of perishables.
