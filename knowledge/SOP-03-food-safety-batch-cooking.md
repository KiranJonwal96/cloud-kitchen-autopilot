## Batch cooking ahead of a peak
Batch-cooked biryani and curry bases may be prepared up to 60 minutes before the expected peak.
Cook no more than the forecast for the next 90 minutes to limit wastage.

## Hot holding
Hold cooked food at 63 °C or above. Discard hot-held biryani and curries after 2 hours. Record the
start time of every batch on the holding label.

## Pre-portioning raw chicken
Raw chicken may be pre-portioned for the next hour of service only and must stay below 5 °C until
cooking. Portioned chicken not used within 2 hours is discarded and recorded as wastage.
