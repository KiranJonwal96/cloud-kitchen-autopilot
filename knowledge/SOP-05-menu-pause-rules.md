## Who can pause
Pausing a dish on delivery platforms changes what customers can order, so it needs supervisor
approval. The system may recommend a pause but never pauses a dish on its own.

## When to pause
Pause a dish when an ingredient will run out before new stock can arrive, or when its station is
far above capacity and orders would breach the delivery promise.

## Resuming
Resume the dish as soon as stock is received or the peak has passed. Paused dishes lower platform
ranking, so pauses should be as short as possible.
