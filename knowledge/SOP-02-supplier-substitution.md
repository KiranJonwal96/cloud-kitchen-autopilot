## Approved suppliers only
Purchase orders may only be placed with suppliers on the approved list. Emergency local purchases
from unapproved vendors are not allowed for meat, dairy or cheese because of cold-chain and food
safety requirements.

## Long lead times
Dairy (paneer, cream) from DairyDirect has a 3-hour lead time and cheese from CheeseHub a 4-hour lead
time. For same-evening shortages of these items, the PO protects the next service; the current
service must be protected by pausing or limiting the affected dishes.

## Chicken
FreshMeat Co delivers within 60 minutes for orders placed before 21:00. Orders after 21:00 arrive
the next morning. Chicken shortages during dinner should be ordered as early as possible.

## Substitutions
Ingredient substitutions that change a dish (for example tofu instead of paneer) are not allowed
without a menu change approved by the head chef, because delivery platforms show fixed dish
descriptions and allergen information.
