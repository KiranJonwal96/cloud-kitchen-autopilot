## When this applies
Use this procedure when forecast consumption plus normal loss is higher than current stock for any
ingredient in the next service window, or when stock will fall below safety stock.

## Stockout risk (stock will run out)
Raise a purchase order immediately with the primary supplier if the delivery lead time is shorter
than the time until the shortage. If the delivery cannot arrive in time, pause the affected menu
items on delivery platforms before the stock runs out, so customers are not accepted for dishes that
cannot be cooked. Inform the shift supervisor and note the time of the pause.

## Safety-stock risk (stock stays positive but drops below safety stock)
Raise a top-up purchase order sized to restore safety stock. Do not pause menu items for a
safety-stock risk alone. Prioritise usage of the oldest batch (first-expiry, first-out).

## Portion control during shortages
Weigh portions for the short ingredient for the rest of the service. Do not reduce published portion
sizes; quality complaints cost more than a short menu pause.
